About

I am a JRG Fellow working with Prof. Patricio Salgado-Rebolledo at the Asia Pacific Center for Theoretical Physics (APCTP) in Pohang, South Korea. My current research includes understanding the holomorphic modular bootstrap for 2d rational conformal field theories (RCFTs), correlation functions in superconformal field theories (SCFTs), and investigations in non-Lorentzian limits of quantum field theories.

I completed my PhD at Ashoka University, India, under the supervision of Prof. Amin Ahmad Nizami. Prior to my postdoc, I was a visitor at IIT Madras working with Prof. Suresh Govindarajan.

Current Research
  • 3-point superconformal invariants and correlators in 3d 𝒩=3,4 SCFTs
    Determining invariant structures and 3-point correlators in 3d 𝒩=3,4 SCFTs
  • Holomorphic bootstrap for supersymmetric RCFTs
    Extension of MMS-classification scheme to 2d RCFTs with super-Virasoro algebra
  • Correlation functions in non-relativistic CFTs
    Implications of non-relativistic conformal symmetry on 2-pt and 3-pt correlators
  • Carrollian/Galilean limits
    Understanding the non-Lorentzian limits of scalar, EM, gravity theories
